Maktab haqida

Soʻzga mehr, bilimga chanqoqlik A love of words, a thirst for knowledge

Alisher Navoi nomidagi oʻzbek tili va adabiyotiga ixtisoslashtirilgan davlat umumtaʼlim maktabi — Navoi shahrida ona tili, adabiyot va xorijiy tillarni chuqur oʻrgatishga yoʻnaltirilgan taʼlim maskani. Alisher Navoi Specialized School in Navoi — a specialized state school devoted to the native language, literature and a strong command of foreign languages.

Bizning maqsadimizOur mission

Oʻquvchilarni ona tiliga mehr, bilimga chanqoqlik va oʻrganishda kamtarlik ruhida tarbiyalash. To raise young people with a love for their native language, a thirst for knowledge and humility in learning.

Maktabimiz buyuk shoir va mutafakkir Alisher Navoi nomini oladi. Bu nom biz uchun katta sharaf va ayni paytda masʼuliyatdir: soʻzning qadriga yetish, kitobni sevish va bilim yoʻlida tinimsiz izlanish.

Bugun maktabimizda 3190 nafar oʻquvchi 110 ta sinfda tahsil oladi, ularga 257 nafar oʻqituvchi saboq beradi. Har bir dars va har bir tadbir bitta maqsadga xizmat qiladi: oʻquvchi fikrini erkin va chiroyli ifoda eta oladigan, dunyoga ochiq va oʻz ildizlarini qadrlaydigan inson boʻlib yetishsin.

Shiorimiz — «Qancha koʻp bilsang, shuncha koʻp bilmasligingni tushunasan» — bu yoʻlning mohiyatini ifodalaydi: haqiqiy bilim kamtarlikka, kamtarlik esa yangi izlanishlarga yetaklaydi.

Our school bears the name of the great poet and thinker Alisher Navoi. For us, that name is both an honour and a responsibility: to value the written word, to love books and to keep searching on the path of knowledge.

Today 3,190 students study in 110 classes, taught by 257 teachers. Every lesson and every event serves one goal — that each student grows into a person who can express their thoughts freely and beautifully, who is open to the world and who values their roots.

Our motto — “The more you know, the more you realize how much you don’t know” — captures the spirit of that journey: true knowledge leads to humility, and humility leads to new discoveries.

TariximizOur history

Qisqa, ammo mazmunli yoʻl A short but meaningful journey

Maktabimiz 2017-yil sentabr oyida 19-sonli maktab sifatida oʻz eshiklarini ochdi.

2019-yilda Xalq taʼlimi vaziri Sherzod Shermatovning tashrifidan soʻng maktabga ixtisoslashtirilgan davlat umumtaʼlim maktabi (IDUM) maqomi berildi va u Alisher Navoi nomi bilan atala boshladi. Shu tariqa maktab oʻzbek tili va adabiyotiga ixtisoslashgan taʼlim maskaniga aylandi.

Our school opened its doors in September 2017 as School No. 19.

In 2019, following a visit by the Minister of Public Education, Sherzod Shermatov, the school was granted the status of a specialized state school (IDUM) and received the name of Alisher Navoi. From then on, it has been a school specializing in the Uzbek language and literature.

Ona tiliga chuqur ildiz, xorijiy tillar orqali dunyoga ochiqlik va xalqaro imtihonlarga puxta tayyorgarlik. Deep roots in the native language, openness to the world through foreign languages, and solid preparation for international exams.

Oʻzbek tili va adabiyotiUzbek language and literature

Maktabimizning asosiy ixtisosligi. Darslar, adabiy kechalar va kitobxonlik orqali oʻquvchilar soʻz sanʼatini chuqur his qilishga oʻrganadi.Our core specialization. Through lessons, literary evenings and reading, students learn to feel the art of the word deeply.
