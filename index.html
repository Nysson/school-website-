Maktab rahbariyatining tabrigi

Hurmatli oʻquvchilar, ota-onalar va mehmonlar! Alisher Navoi nomidagi ixtisoslashtirilgan maktabimiz saytiga xush kelibsiz.

Maktabimiz buyuk mutafakkir nomini gʻurur bilan olib yuradi. Biz oʻquvchilarimizda ona tili va adabiyotiga mehrni, xorijiy tillarni puxta egallash ishtiyoqini va eng muhimi — oʻrganishdan toʻxtamaslik odatini tarbiyalashga intilamiz.

Shiorimizda aytilganidek, bilim ortgan sari inson oʻzi hali bilmagan narsalarni ham teranroq anglaydi. Aynan shu qiziquvchanlik va kamtarlik oʻquvchilarimizni yangi marralar sari yetaklaydi.

Sizni maktabimiz hayoti bilan yaqindan tanishishga taklif etamiz.

Dear students, parents and guests — welcome to the website of the Alisher Navoi Specialized School in Navoi.

Our school proudly bears the name of a great poet and thinker. We aim to nurture in our students a love for their native language and literature, a drive to master foreign languages and, above all, the habit of never stopping learning.

As our motto reminds us, the more we learn, the more clearly we see how much remains to be discovered. It is this blend of curiosity and humility that carries our students towards new achievements.

We warmly invite you to get to know our school.

Safarova Nargishon
